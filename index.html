Mewzaic: Cat Color Puzzle

Cats throw yarn at colorful cubes. Clear the board and collect pixel pictures.

Coming soon to Google Play

Mewzaic is a cozy color-sort puzzle where the cats do the shooting. Pick a cat, send it onto the belt and watch it throw yarn at every cube of its own color. Clear the board to reveal a pixel picture and add it to your gallery.

Screenshots

How to play

  • Tap the cat at the front of a column to send it onto the belt.
  • A cat only throws yarn at cubes of its own color, so the order matters.
  • A cat that still has yarn left waits on the bench. The bench has only a few spots, so never let it fill up!
  • Clear every cube to win. Win without boosters or continues for three stars.

What you will find

  • 200 levels in 20 worlds, from relaxing starters to real brain-teasers. Every level was checked to be winnable.
  • Tough cubes that need two hits, and mystery cats that only show their color once they reach the front.
  • A gallery of pixel pictures to collect, and hats to dress up your cats.
  • Boosters for when you get stuck: Extra Slot, Undo and Color Blast.
  • A daily gift and a Season Pass with a free reward track.
  • No account and no sign-in: your progress is saved on your device.
  • Plays offline and speaks ten languages, chosen by your phone's language: English, Español, Português, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Türkçe, 日本語, 한국어 and Bahasa Indonesia.

Free to play

Mewzaic is free to download and play. It offers optional in-app purchases for real money: coins, booster bundles, a Season Pass and a VIP subscription. The VIP subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in Google Play. Coins and boosters are virtual items that can only be used in this game.
